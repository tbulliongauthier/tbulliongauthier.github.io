**Affine Sobolev inequalities**

Zhang proved the affine Sobolev inequality
\\[||f||_{L^{N/(N-1)}}\\ \leq C_{1,p} \mathcal{E}_{1,1}(f)\coloneq \left( \int_{\mathbb{S}^{N-1}} \left( ||\nabla f \cdot \xi ||\right)^{-N} \ d\xi \right)^{-1/N}, \ \forall \  f \in W^{1,1}. \ \text{(1)} \\] 
This inequality is a refinement of the classical Sobolev inequality and it is invariant under unimodular transformations. 

The work of Zhang inspired many subsequent results, including, for example,  \\(L^p\\) affine Sobolev (Lutwak-Yang-Zhang) and Moser-Trudinger inequalities (Lutwak, Cianchi, Yang, Zhang), as well as stability results on affine Polya-Szego inequalities (Wang, Nguyen). A common trait of these contributions is their use of convex geometry techniques.

Recently, Haddad and Ludwig, using tools of classical analysis,  established the following fractional counterparts of (1) 
\\[|f|_{Np/(N-sp)} \leq C_{s,p} \mathcal{E} \coloneq \int_{\mathbb{S}^{N-1}} \left( ||\nabla f \cdot \xi ||\right)^{-N/sp} \ d\xi \right)^{-1/Ns}, \ \forall \ f \in W^{s,p}. \\]

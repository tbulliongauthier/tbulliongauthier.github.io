I taught the following courses at Université Lyon 1: 

- Analysis 2 (exercise sessions). Basics of real analysis (continuity, differentiability, taylor expansions, integrals, differential equations).
- Maths 3 (exercise sessions). Basics of analysis and linear algebra for physics students (sequences, series, differential equations, linear systems, reduction).
- Techniques mathématiques de base (exercise sessions). Basic tools in mathematics for physics students (complex numbers, functions of the real variable, integrals, differential equations).

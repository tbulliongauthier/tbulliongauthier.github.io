I taught the following courses at Université Lyon 1: 

- Analysis 2 (exercice sessions). Basics of real analysis (continuity, differentiability, taylor expansions, integrals, differential equations).
- Maths 3 (exercice sessions). Basics of analysis and linear algebra for physics students (sequences, series, differential equations, reduction...).
- Techniques mathématiques de base (exercise sessions). Basic tools in mathematics for physics students (complex numbers, functions of the real variable, integrals, differential equations...).
